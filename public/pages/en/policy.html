General Introduction

Welcome to Readbox — the smart reading application that helps you discover, read, and manage your personal book library. We are committed to protecting your privacy and personal data.

This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use the Readbox application on iOS, Android platforms, and related services.

Data Sources

Book sources on Readbox are aggregated from various open-source platforms, user contributions, and the internet. Therefore, Readbox is not responsible for the copyright of the book content.

However, if you are the copyright owner and find that your content is being used without authorization, please contact us via email at support@readbox.vn so we can remove the content.

If you have the financial means, please purchase books to support the authors and publishers.
Important Note

By using the Readbox application, you agree to the terms described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue using the application.

02

Data We Collect

2.1. Information You Provide Directly

Data Type Details Required
Account Information Full name, email, avatar, date of birth Required
Social Login Information from Google, Facebook, Apple ID Optional
Payment Information Subscription transaction history (processed via PayOS) Optional
User Content Uploaded books, notes, bookmarks, highlights Optional

2.2. Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system, app version, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: App opening frequency, reading duration, read books, reading progress
  • Location Information: We do not collect your precise location data
  • Notification Token (FCM): Used to send push notifications for new books and app updates

2.3. Biometric Data

Readbox supports biometric authentication (fingerprint, Face ID) to unlock the app. Biometric data is processed entirely on your device and is never sent to our servers.

03

Purposes of Data Processing

We use your data for the following purposes:

  • Service Provision: Creating and managing accounts, syncing book library, saving reading progress
  • Experience Improvement: Recommending relevant books based on reading history and preferences
  • AI Features: Utilizing Gemini AI for vocabulary lookups, translations, and reading comprehension support
  • Notifications: Sending updates about new books, app updates, and subscription offers
  • Document Conversion: Converting book formats (PDF, EPUB, etc.) to ensure compatibility with the app
  • Payment: Processing Premium subscription transactions
  • Security: Detecting and preventing fraudulent activities and unauthorized access
  • Legal Compliance: Meeting legal requirements as necessary
✅ Our Commitment

We do not sell your personal data to third parties or use it for targeted advertising.

04

Legal Basis for Data Processing

We process your personal data based on the following legal grounds:

  • Consent: When you register an account and accept this policy
  • Contract Performance: To deliver the reading services and features you request
  • Legitimate Interests: To improve service quality and ensure system safety
  • Legal Obligations: To comply with Vietnamese and international data protection laws

This policy complies with Decree No. 13/2023/ND-CP on personal data protection by the Government of Vietnam, as well as equivalent regulations under applicable laws.

06

Data Security

We implement various security measures to protect your data:

🔐 Technical Measures

  • Transit Encryption: All data is encrypted using TLS/SSL during transit
  • Storage Encryption: Passwords are stored in hashed format (bcrypt) and cannot be reverse-decrypted
  • JWT Authentication: JSON Web Tokens with expiration times are used to secure login sessions
  • Biometric Authentication: Support for fingerprint and Face ID for additional account protection
  • CORS Protection: Only allows access from approved origins

🛡️ Organizational Measures

  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
  • Monitoring and logging system access
  • Regular security assessments
  • Security awareness training for staff
🔒 Maximum Security

We never store plain-text passwords, payment card information, or biometric data on our servers.

07

Data Retention and Deletion

7.1. Retention Period

  • Account Data: Retained for as long as you use the services
  • Reading History: Retained to sync progress across devices
  • Payment Data: Retained as required by law (up to 5 years)
  • System Logs: Automatically deleted after 90 days

7.2. Data Deletion

When you request to delete your account:

  • Personal data will be deleted within 30 days
  • Uploaded books and documents will be permanently deleted
  • Certain data may be retained in an anonymized format for aggregate statistical purposes
  • Data required to be retained by law will be stored separately and securely

User Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Right Description
Right to Access View and download a copy of your personal data
Right to Rectification Update or modify inaccurate personal information
Right to Erasure Request deletion of your account and all associated data
Right to Restrict Processing Request a temporary pause on data processing under certain conditions

To exercise the above rights, please contact us via email or through the "Support Center" section in the application. We will respond within 15 business days.

09

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

As a mobile application, Readbox does not use traditional cookies. However, we utilize similar technologies to improve your experience:

  • Firebase Analytics: Collects anonymous usage data to analyze and improve the application
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: Manages push notification tokens
  • Shared Preferences / UserDefaults: Stores local settings and preferences on your device

You can manage your notification preferences in the Settings section of the app.

10

Children's Privacy

Readbox is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

If you are a parent or guardian and discover that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can delete that data.

For children between 13 and 16 years old, using the application requires the consent and supervision of a parent or legal guardian.
